Description

Open neural-network control libraries for RSCAD/RTDS enabling real-time training and adaptive HVDC converter control on NovaCor 1.0 platforms. Demonstrated for adaptive PI MMC control and tightly linked to the group’s machine-learning-in-the-loop research narrative.
