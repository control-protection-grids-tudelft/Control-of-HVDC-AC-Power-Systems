Description

An open RSCAD/RTDS model library of futuristic multi-terminal HVDC / energy-hub systems with ratings aligned to 2 GW, ±525 kV designs and adjusted CIGRE B4 parameters based on TenneT substation and cable practice. Built for NovaCor 1.0 and used in teaching, research and neural-network control libraries.

Referenced in CIGRE B4 contexts and EU project PROSECCO interaction and protection studies.
