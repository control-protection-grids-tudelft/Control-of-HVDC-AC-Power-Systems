Description

Aleksandra Lekić is responsible instructor and teaches about one third of the IEEE IES MOOC “Control and protection of HVDC/AC electrical grids”, co-financed by SUNRISE and IEEE IES, with 15 lecturers from academia and industry.

The free Resource Center course is backed by open RSCAD/RTDS and MATLAB model libraries. Completing the course and end quiz yields 74 PDH (≈3 ECTS). The material fills a gap seldom covered at universities at this depth for both academia and industry.
