Description

DCSS is a switchyard controller providing communication between IEDs and a modular connection point for expandable multi-terminal HVDC grids — complementing PACS (bay/converter C&P) and DCGC (grid control applications).

Status & upgrade plans

  • Switchyard controller concept for modular multi-terminal HVDC expansion
  • Complements PACS and DCGC in the Interoperability Program hardware stack
