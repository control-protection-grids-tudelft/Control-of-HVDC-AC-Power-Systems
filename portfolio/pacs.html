Description

PACS packages hierarchical control and protection on FPGA hardware as part of PROSECCO demonstrator work. It is the hardware embodiment of the Interoperability Program’s C&P software stack for DC relays and converter-near protection/control units in HIL and substation contexts.

Status & upgrade plans

  • FPGA-based hierarchical control and protection within PROSECCO
  • Demonstrated toward DC relay and converter-near C&P units in HIL
