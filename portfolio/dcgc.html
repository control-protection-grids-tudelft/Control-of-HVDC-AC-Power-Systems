Description

DCGC aims to act as a digital substation / control-centre layer for DC grids: modular provision of SCADA, EMS-like grid calculations, load-frequency control, scheduling and related functions with interoperability by design.

Architecture builds on the LF Energy SEAPATH open-source substation stack with ACDC-OpFlow integrated — matching the RTDS–Ethernet–SEAPATH laboratory architecture already exercised in the group.

Status & upgrade plans
