### SLIDE 54 imgs=['image116.png']
Hybrid Control strategy for Modern MMC-based HVDC systems Why? Grid short circuit ratio is becoming more unpredictable. To merge GFL and GFM advantages and make their demerits insignificant. How? Using the concept of polytopic Lyapunov functions to define switching boundaries, which makes this control and the system inherently large signal stable. Applications? To combat the sudden SCR variations in the AC grid connected to the HVDC network to ensure uninterrupted and stable power transmission. To mitigate faults and other unexpected transient phenomena by rapidly switching between GFL and GFM as and when required, as defined by the PLF polytopes. [3] R . Kamat Tarcar, M. Popov, and A. Lekić, “Polytopic Lyapunov Function-Based Hybrid Switching Control Strategy for High Voltage Direct Current Networks ”, IEEE Open Access Journal of Power and Energy, 2025.
