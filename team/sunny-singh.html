Postdoctoral researcher on SAFE-GRID (NWO Veni), developing data-driven model predictive control that brings PE converter execution toward the microsecond range for stable multi-terminal, multi-vendor HVDC operation under large disturbances. B.Sc./M.Sc. Applied Mathematics (DDU Gorakhpur, 2014/2016); Ph.D. Mathematics, IIT (BHU) Varanasi (2024). Research interests: nonlinear dynamics, control theory, neural networks and system identification.
