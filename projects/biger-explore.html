BiGER Explore

Bridging the Gap between EMT and RMS – Explore

Overview

PE-based components introduce fast dynamics that interact with classical slow electromechanical modes and among converters themselves. Converter-driven stability (e.g. PLL or control gains) can appear before traditional issues, especially in weak networks — requiring more than classical phasor studies, while full long-term EMT remains computationally prohibitive.

Project description

BiGER–Explore builds and shares open-source benchmarks illustrating the need for more detailed simulations, and a state-of-the-art review of methods that approach EMT fidelity with better scalability, robustness, transparency and flexibility for network operators and stakeholders.

Objectives & deliverables

  • Build and share open-source use cases and benchmarks needing beyond-RMS simulation
  • State-of-the-art of methods bridging EMT and RMS for converter-driven stability

Partners

TU Delft, RWTH Aachen and RTE under the CRESYM organisation.

TU Delft role

TU Delft lead with Pedro P. Vergara Barrios, Aleksandra Lekić and Rashmi Prasad contributing expertise on converter-interfaced device stability in large networks.
